place — your workspaces follow your monitors

One small daemon watches your niri session. Every physical monitor arrangement you connect is learned once and re-created on every return: all workspaces form one strip, and each setup is just a different cut of it.

Architecture

The 5-second loop

read niri state→ identify setup→ new? register + spawn overlay changed? restore split → converge → refocus same? learn your rearranging

Locked session? Transitions are deferred until unlock. place.py pause silences everything; resume re-applies.

    Data stores & ownership

    filecontentowner
    place/place.json every known setup: monitor-set hash, clicked desk order, id, role order, “arranged” flag place (auto)
    ~/.local/state/place/state.json live state: workspace strip, block sizes, per-setup splits (survive reboots) place (auto, disposable)
    niri conf/output.kdl physical monitor pins: position, scale, mode you — place never reads or writes it

    place reads geometry from niri’s runtime, i.e. after your config was applied. Your clicked desk order is the authoritative arrangement key — niri’s auto-layout is only a provisional guess.

    Glossary

    setup — one physical monitor arrangement; id = hash of the clicked left→right order
    desk order — monitors left→right on your desk, defined by clicking (pass 1)
    role / primary — importance order (pass 2); role 0 absorbs new workspaces and keeps focus
    split — per-setup memory of how many workspaces live on each monitor
    strip — all workspaces in one canonical order; every setup is a different cut of it
    converge — send & verify moves until reality matches the plan
